**Symptom:** Not applicable — this is a reference list of parts commonly needed when servicing small industrial electric motors (roughly NEMA 48-215T frame or IEC 63-132 frame range), not a fault diagnosis.

**Likely cause:** Not applicable.

**How to confirm:** Not applicable — consult this list when planning a repair to identify which parts to have on hand before starting work.

**Fix (reference list):**
- Ball bearings, both drive-end and non-drive-end (bore/OD/width vary by frame size; always match manufacturer part number, not just dimensions).
- Bearing grease, NLGI grade 2 lithium-complex or manufacturer-specified equivalent.
- Shaft seals / V-rings to prevent contamination ingress at the bearing.
- Start and run capacitors (single-phase motors), matched by µF rating and voltage class.
- Cooling fan (external, plastic or aluminum) and fan shroud/cover.
- Terminal box gasket and cover fasteners.
- Mounting bolts, lock washers, and shims for foot-mounted motors.
- Overload relay heater elements or electronic overload modules matched to nameplate FLA.
- Coupling inserts/spiders for flexible couplings, sized to the coupling model.

**Parts/tools:** Motor nameplate data (frame size, FLA, bearing numbers if listed) for correct part cross-referencing before ordering.
