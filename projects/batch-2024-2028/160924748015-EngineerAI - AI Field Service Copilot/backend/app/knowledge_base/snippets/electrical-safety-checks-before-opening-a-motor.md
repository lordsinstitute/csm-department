**Symptom:** Not applicable — these are mandatory pre-work checks to perform before opening a motor's terminal box, end bells, or any enclosure, after lockout-tagout has already been applied.

**Likely cause:** Not applicable.

**How to confirm:** Not applicable — perform the checks below every time, regardless of how routine the task appears.

**Fix (procedure):**
1. Confirm lockout-tagout is already in place and verified per the standard procedure; do not proceed on the assumption that "it's probably off."
2. Test the voltage tester itself on a known energized source immediately before use to confirm it is functioning.
3. Test for absence of voltage at the motor terminals, phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground, before touching any internal wiring.
4. Discharge any capacitors present (start/run capacitors, VFD DC bus if applicable) with an insulated discharge tool — capacitors can retain a dangerous charge well after power is removed.
5. Inspect for unexpected backfeed sources: standby generators, VFDs, or control circuits powered from a separate source than the main disconnect.
6. Wear appropriate PPE (insulated gloves rated for the voltage present, safety glasses) while performing verification steps, even though the circuit is expected to be de-energized.
7. Only after all checks pass should covers, terminal boxes, or end bells be opened for inspection or repair.

**Parts/tools:** Rated voltage tester, insulated discharge tool, insulated gloves, safety glasses.
