**Symptom:** Not applicable — this is a mandatory safety procedure to perform before any physical inspection, adjustment, or repair on a motor, not a fault diagnosis.

**Likely cause:** Not applicable.

**How to confirm:** Not applicable — follow the procedure below regardless of confirmed diagnosis.

**Fix (procedure):**
1. Identify all energy sources for the motor: electrical supply, and any stored mechanical energy (spring tension, elevated loads, residual rotation).
2. Notify affected personnel that the equipment is being taken out of service.
3. Shut down the motor using its normal stopping procedure, then open the disconnect switch or remove/rack out the breaker.
4. Apply a personal lock and tag to the disconnect or breaker. Each technician working on the equipment applies their own lock — never work on equipment locked out only by someone else.
5. Verify zero energy state: attempt to start the motor using the normal start control to confirm it does not run, then test for absence of voltage at the motor terminals with a rated multimeter, verifying the meter itself on a known live source before and after.
6. Discharge any capacitors (e.g., start capacitors) before touching related circuitry.
7. Only after verification is complete may work begin. Locks are removed only by the technician who applied them, after confirming the equipment is safe to re-energize.

**Parts/tools:** Personal lock and tag, rated voltage tester, disconnect/breaker lockout device, insulated discharge tool.
