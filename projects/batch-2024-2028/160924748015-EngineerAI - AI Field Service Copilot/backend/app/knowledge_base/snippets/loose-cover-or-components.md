---
title: "Loose Cover or Components"
problem_tags: ["Noise"]
source: "EngineerAI KB"
---

**Symptom:** A rattling, buzzing, or intermittent tapping noise that changes or disappears when light hand pressure is applied to the fan shroud, terminal box cover, or other external hardware. Often more noticeable at certain speeds due to resonance, and typically not accompanied by any rise in temperature or current.

**Likely cause:** External hardware — the fan shroud, terminal (conduit) box cover, nameplate, or a mounting foot cover — has a loose fastener and is vibrating or resonating against the housing at running speed. This is a cosmetic/mechanical nuisance fault rather than an internal electrical or bearing issue, but it can be mistaken for a more serious problem if not checked first.

**How to confirm:** With the motor running (and guarding in place per safety procedure), listen for changes in the noise as you lightly press on the shroud, terminal box, and access covers one at a time — a noise that stops when a specific component is pressed identifies the source. With power locked out, visually inspect and hand-check all external fasteners for looseness.

**Fix:** Tighten or replace the loose fastener(s) to the manufacturer's specified torque. If a component (shroud, cover) is cracked or its mounting boss is stripped, replace the part rather than over-tightening a damaged fastener point, which will loosen again quickly.

**Parts/tools:** Assorted screwdrivers/wrenches matching fastener types, torque wrench, replacement fasteners or covers as needed.
