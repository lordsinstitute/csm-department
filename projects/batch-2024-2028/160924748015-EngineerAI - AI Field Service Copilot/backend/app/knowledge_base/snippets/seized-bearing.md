---
title: "Seized Bearing"
problem_tags: ["Won't Start"]
source: "EngineerAI KB"
---

**Symptom:** Motor hums or the overload trips immediately on start attempt, and the shaft cannot be turned by hand at all (or only with significant force) when power is locked out. No amount of electrical troubleshooting resolves it, since the fault is purely mechanical.

**Likely cause:** A bearing has completely seized — rolling elements have fused, corroded, or the grease has hardened solid — usually the end stage of long-term bearing wear or contamination that went unaddressed, or from a motor sitting idle and unused in a humid environment long enough for internal corrosion to lock the bearing.

**How to confirm:** With power locked out, attempt to rotate the shaft by hand. A bearing-seizure fault will not turn at all, or will turn with a very high, uneven resistance rather than the light drag of a merely worn bearing. Remove the end bells if needed to isolate whether the seizure is at the bearing versus a rotor-to-stator rub from a different cause.

**Fix:** Replace the seized bearing(s) entirely — do not attempt to free a seized bearing with heat or lubricant and return it to service, as internal damage (spalling, flat-spotting) is already present. Inspect the shaft journal for scoring caused by the seizure before pressing on the new bearing, and re-check free rotation before re-energizing.

**Parts/tools:** Replacement bearing(s), bearing puller, induction heater or press, specified grease.
