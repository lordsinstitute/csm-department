**Symptom:** Not applicable — this is a reference list of the standard toolkit for field diagnosis and repair of small industrial electric motors, not a fault diagnosis.

**Likely cause:** Not applicable.

**How to confirm:** Not applicable — consult this list to ensure the right tools are on hand before starting a job.

**Fix (reference list):**
- Digital multimeter (voltage, current, resistance, continuity).
- Clamp meter for running current measurement without breaking the circuit.
- Megohmmeter (megger) for insulation resistance testing, 500V DC range for low-voltage motors.
- Dial indicator and magnetic base, or laser shaft alignment kit.
- Torque wrench covering the bolt sizes used on mounting feet and coupling hardware.
- Bearing puller set (external and internal jaw types) and an induction bearing heater or hydraulic press.
- Insulated hand tool set (screwdrivers, pliers, wrenches) rated for electrical work.
- Infrared thermometer for non-contact temperature checks.
- Mechanic's stethoscope or a long screwdriver for bearing noise localization.
- Lockout-tagout kit: personal locks, tags, and a rated voltage tester.
- Assorted shim stock, feeler gauges, and grease gun with specified grease.

**Parts/tools:** This list itself is the reference; verify each item's rating (voltage, load) matches the motor and voltage class being serviced before use.
