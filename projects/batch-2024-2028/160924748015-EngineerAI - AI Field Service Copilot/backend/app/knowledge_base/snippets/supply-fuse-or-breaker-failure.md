---
title: "Supply, Fuse, or Breaker Failure"
problem_tags: ["Won't Start"]
source: "EngineerAI KB"
---

**Symptom:** Motor is completely dead — no hum, no movement, no vibration — when the start command is given. Control circuit indicator lights (if present) may also be unlit, suggesting no power is reaching the starter at all.

**Likely cause:** Loss of incoming power to the motor circuit from a blown fuse, a tripped or failed circuit breaker, a disconnect switch left open, or a failed contactor that never closes. This is the most basic and most common won't-start cause and should always be checked first before investigating the motor itself.

**How to confirm:** With appropriate PPE, verify the disconnect switch is in the closed/on position. Check fuses for continuity with a multimeter (a good fuse reads near-zero ohms) or visually inspect for a blown element. Check the breaker position — a tripped breaker often sits in a middle position between on and off. Measure voltage at the motor starter's line side terminals to confirm supply is present, then at the load side to confirm the contactor is actually closing.

**Fix:** Reset the breaker or replace the blown fuse with the correct rating (never a higher rating than specified). If the breaker trips again immediately, do not force it — investigate for a downstream short or overload before re-energizing. Replace a contactor that fails to pull in when commanded.

**Parts/tools:** Multimeter, replacement fuses (correct rating), appropriate electrical PPE, contactor if faulty.
